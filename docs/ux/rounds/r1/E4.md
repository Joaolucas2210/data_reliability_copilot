### Verified strengths
- axe found **0 violations** on every loaded screen in both themes, viewports and roles, apart from the issues in F13, F16 and F19. There were no colour-contrast violations anywhere, including pills, epistemic tags, muted text, banners, and error/denied states.
- Focus ring: 3 px solid `--focus` (#1d4ed8 light / #8ab4ff dark) with a 2 px offset at every stop observed. This covers links, selects, tabs, the tabpanel, cite chips, table regions and buttons, including the primary button in dark (`shots/J10_gallery_dark_primary_focus.png`).
- Tabs follow the APG pattern: roving tabindex, ←/→/Home/End verified, tabpanel focusable (`shots/kbd_tabpanel_focus.png`).
- A citation chip moves focus to its evidence article, with a visible ring (`shots/kbd_chip_to_evidence.png`).
- Status and epistemic tags never rely on colour alone; each has an icon, text and a border shape (`shots/J10_gallery_dark_states.png`).
- With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the spinner stops (`animationName: none`). It is the only motion in the app.
- At 200 % zoom no screen overflows horizontally. Wide tables sit in focusable, labelled scroll regions.
- Errors use `role=alert` with a Retry button; loading uses `role=status`.
