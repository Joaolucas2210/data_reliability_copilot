## 1. Scores per criterion with evidence

### Nielsen
- **N1 Visibility of system status: 8.**
  - Good: status pills on every entity. Investigation and validation pages show "updates automatically · last checked HH:MM:SS UTC" (S/f_j4_t00.png, S/c_j6_running.png). Buttons show busy labels with a spinner ("Starting…", S/f_j4_starting.png). Loading states use a spinner plus text (S/s_loading_*).
  - Problem: the sandbox validation ran for about 75 s (log 17:28:08→17:29:23). During that whole time all 6 stages showed "pending", with no per-stage progress, until the run finished (S/c_j6_running.png).
- **N2 Match with the real world: 6.** Internal codes leak into the UI:
  - Evaluations headers are snake_case keys (`cost_usd_total`, `appropriate_abstention_rate`; S/c_m_light_1024_evals.png).
  - Usage card shows `limit max_cost_usd`.
  - Meta line reads "fake/—".
  - Wording oddities: "Proposal proposed" (S/s_viewer_rec_light_1440.png), "Cancelled by dev:investigator", "Cost unknown (unknown)".
  - Raw JSON blobs appear as validation step details (S/c_val1440.png).
  - Check `inv_payment_id_unique` shows Observed 0 / Expected 0 / Failures 14 (S/combo_j4.png), which contradicts itself to a finance owner.
- **N3 User control and freedom: 8.**
  - Good: two-step confirm with "Go back". Cancel of a queued investigation works (S/combo_j8.png). Browser back/forward restores the routes (log: back → incident, forward → investigation).
  - Problem: switching role reloads the page and silently resets the theme chosen in the session (log: theme `dark` → `undefined`/System after the role switch and after a reload).
  - Problem: after jumping from a citation chip to its evidence card, there is no way back to the claim.
- **N4 Consistency and standards: 7.**
  - Status pills are lowercase in the gallery ("pass", "fail") but Title Case in the app ("Pass", "Fail").
  - The page-not-found state is left-aligned, while the NotFound/Empty/Error states are centred (S/combo_states390.png).
  - The breadcrumb last item shows the raw id on error/loading but "Incident" when loaded.
  - In Business invariants, "Before" is plain text while "After holds" is a pill.
  - In key-value lists, mono 14 px values and sans 16 px values are mixed in one list (Evidence "Collected" is larger than its neighbours; S/c_inv_mid.png).
  - The gallery h1 is not focusable, unlike every other page.
- **N5 Error prevention: 9.**
  - Good: confirmation before irreversible decisions. A note is required to reject; the guard focuses the textarea and shows an inline error (S/combo_j7.png). There is an idempotency key on start, and a confirm before starting a parallel investigation.
  - Problem: while the Approve confirm is open, "Reject diagnosis" stays active beside it (S/combo_j7.png, third panel).
- **N6 Recognition rather than recall: 8.**
  - Good: breadcrumbs, labelled key-value lists, and tab labels with counts.
  - Problem: citation chips show only opaque hashes (`ev_57630a23b7e1800f`) with no kind or source hint. To see what a chip refers to, the user jumps about 3,000 px down the page.
- **N7 Flexibility and efficiency: 6.**
  - The incidents list has 39 rows with no search, sort, pagination or dataset/severity filter (S/m_light_1440_incidents.png).
  - The evaluations comparison table is 4,021 px wide inside a 1,102 px box at 1440 (matrix-metrics), so comparing systems needs horizontal scrolling and has no sticky first column.
  - The 19 evidence cards cannot be collapsed or filtered.
- **N8 Aesthetic and minimalist design: 6.**
  - The investigation page is 7,222 px tall at 1440 and 14,986 px at 390.
  - Queued and cancelled investigations still render a stack of empty dashed boxes (Citation check, Evidence (0), Tool calls; S/combo_j4.png, S/combo_j8.png).
  - Raw JSON blocks dominate the validation Steps card (S/c_val1440.png, S/c_j6_res390a.png).
- **N9 Recognise, diagnose, recover from errors: 9.**
  - Good: human-readable 500, abort and 404 messages with Retry or navigation links (S/combo_states390.png; log of the abort text).
  - Problem: an isolation-check detail is truncated mid-word ("ection failed: connection…"), and another starts with a stray leading space (S/c_val1440.png).
- **N10 Help and documentation: 8.**
  - Good: inline explanations (what each sandbox step does and does not prove, the caveat on citation validity, the FAKE LLM banner) and a component gallery.
  - Problem: no help or glossary for terms such as "abstained", "invariant", or the metric names.

### Shneiderman
- **S1 Consistency: 7.** Same issues as N4.
- **S2 Universal usability: 6.**
  - Good: axe finds 0 violations on 6 of 7 screens in both themes, and no page has horizontal overflow at any width.
  - Problem: keyboard and screen-reader users lose focus to BODY after every navigation into a detail page (finding F1).
  - Problem: at 768 px the incidents table is crushed, and at 320 px words break mid-token (F3, F6).
- **S3 Informative feedback: 8.** Announcements, busy states and the inline "⊘ Requires the investigator role" note are good. The 75 s validation gives no per-stage progress.
- **S4 Dialogs that yield closure: 8.**
  - Good: after approval, focus moves to "Decision recorded" and a decision card shows reviewer, time and note (S/combo_j7.png, log `H2:Decision recorded`). Cancel ends in a clear "Investigation cancelled" card.
  - Problem: when validation finishes, the page gives no "back to investigation / next: human review" call to action; only the breadcrumb leads back.
- **S5 Error prevention: 9.** Same as N5.
- **S6 Easy reversal: 8.** Decisions are irreversible by design, and the UI says so up front. Cancel and back work; nothing can be undone after a confirm.
- **S7 User in control: 7.** A role switch forces a full reload and loses the theme. Focus is moved away from where the user acted on detail pages.
- **S8 Short-term memory load: 6.**
  - Claim and evidence are thousands of pixels apart.
  - In the 25-column eval table, the row identity scrolls away.
  - Ids are 20-character hashes the user must match by eye: chip vs evidence card, and incident vs investigation id in the banner.

### UI
- **U1 Visual hierarchy: 7.**
  - Good: clear h1/h2 scale, the status row under the title, the warning banner on top (S/c_inv_top.png).
  - Problem: on the investigation page the 19 evidence cards visually outweigh the diagnosis.
  - Problem: the Access-denied block for non-approvers is a large grey panel, heavier than the actual content (S/c_inv_top.png, "Human review").
- **U2 Typography and legibility: 6.**

- **U3 Spacing and alignment: 6.** Several places have no gap:
  - The "Cancel investigation" button sits flush against the dashed state box (S/combo_j4.png, S/combo_j8.png).
  - The citation caveat sits flush under the empty box (S/combo_j4.png).
  - The proposal row nearly touches the next card (S/s_viewer_rec_light_1440.png, S/f_j7_rec_after.png).

  - The gallery token label "--recommendation-fg" wraps below its swatch, out of the grid's alignment (S/c_gal1.png).
- **U4 Component consistency: 7.** See N4. Also, the gallery does not show button variants (primary, danger, disabled-with-reason), the inline confirm, the invalid textarea, the decision panel or the refresh-error banner, so it is not a complete inventory of component states.
- **U5 Responsiveness: 6.**
  - Good: document scrollWidth equals clientWidth at all 5 widths, both themes, all screens (matrix-metrics.json). Tables stack under 600 px.
  - Problem: from 601 to 959 px the tables neither stack nor fit. At 768 the Incident column is about 70 px wide, with one word per line, and "Opened" is clipped (S/combo_tablet.png).
  - Problem: at 1024 the long check name pushes Status, Observed, Expected and Failures out of view behind a scroll with no affordance (S/combo_tablet.png).
  - Problem: on mobile, the validation steps grid keeps an `auto` status column and squeezes the content (S/c_j6_res390a.png).
  - Problem: on mobile the active nav item uses an inset left bar on a horizontal chip, and the tabs wrap onto two rows (S/c_inv390_top_dark.png).
- **U6 Contrast and colour: 9.**
  - Good: axe colour-contrast passes in light and dark. Dark-theme parity is good (S/c_inv_top_dark.png, S/c_gal1_dark.png). Status and epistemic tags pair icon, text and border shape, never colour alone.
  - Problem: the disabled primary button uses opacity 0.6, which reads as washed-out in dark (S/s_viewer_inc_dark_390.png). This is minor.
- **U7 Keyboard, focus and semantics: 6.**
  - Good: skip link first, visible 3 px focus ring in both themes, WAI-ARIA tabs with arrow keys and Home/End, and citation chips that move focus to the evidence article (S/combo_kbd.png, S/k_dark_evidencefocus.png).
  - Problem: focus is lost to BODY after navigating to any detail page (P1).
  - Problem: static "Access denied" blocks use `role="alert"` on page load.
  - Problem: axe reports `landmark-unique` ×7 in the gallery.
- **U8 Clarity of copy: 7.**
  - Good: the explanations are generally precise.
  - Problems: "Proposal proposed", "Cost unknown (unknown)", "fake/—", snake_case metric headers. The empty filter says "Deterministic checks have not opened any incident", which is wrong for manual reports.
- **U9 Feedback and micro-interactions: 8.** Busy labels, the inline confirm, inline field error with focus, and focus to the decision are good. The evidence target is not highlighted after the focus ring moves on.
- **U10 Motion and perceived performance: 8.**
  - Good: under `reducedMotion: reduce` the spinner animation is `none` (log), and scrolling is not smooth-animated.
  - Problem: perceived performance of the 75 s validation is weak (all "pending").

## 4. Findings

**F1 (P1)**
- Screen/state: all detail pages (incident, investigation, validation) after SPA navigation.
- Problem: focus is lost to BODY. App focuses the loading-state h1, which then unmounts when data arrives.
- Principles: U7, S2, N4.
- Evidence: `kbd2.mjs` log "incident link early: H1:Incident | after: BODY", and the same after Enter on an investigation link and after Start investigation (`flows.mjs` log `focused: BODY`). Static pages keep H1 focus.
- Impact: keyboard and screen-reader users land at the top of the document after every drill-down.
- Fix: render a single persistent h1, or re-focus the h1 once `load.state` becomes ok when focus is on body.

**F2 (P2)**
- Screen/state: `#/evaluations` at every width.
- Problem: the comparison table has 25+ metric columns as headers. It is 4,021 px wide (in 1,102 px at 1440), has no sticky System column, shows raw snake_case keys, and right-aligns text values ("measured") as numbers.
- Principles: N7, S8, N2, U5.
- Evidence: S/c_evals1440_dark.png, S/c_m_light_1024_evals.png, matrix-metrics.
- Impact: J9 comparison requires horizontal scrolling and memory.
- Fix: transpose (metrics as rows, systems as columns), use human labels with units, group the metrics, and right-align only numeric cells.

**F3 (P2)**
- Screen/state: tables at 601–1023 px (incidents, triggering checks, gallery tables).
- Problem: the tables neither stack nor fit. At 768 the Incident column is about 70 px wide with one word per line, and Opened is clipped. At 1024, Status/Observed/Expected/Failures are scrolled off-screen with no affordance.
- Principles: U5, U2.
- Evidence: S/combo_tablet.png; matrix-metrics `table-wrap sw 1075 / cw 706`.
- Impact: tablet and narrow-desktop triage is hard, and the most important column (Status) is hidden.
- Fix: raise the stack breakpoint to about 960 px or hide low-priority columns. Truncate long check names with a tooltip. Add a scroll-shadow affordance.

**F4 (P2)**
- Screen/state: investigation page (1440 and 390).
- Problem: very long page (7,222 / 14,986 px). 19 uncollapsed evidence cards; claims and cited evidence are thousands of pixels apart, with no way back from evidence to claim.
- Principles: N8, S8, U1.
- Evidence: S/m_light_1440_inv.png, S/m_dark_390_inv.png, S/k_dark_evidencefocus.png.
- Impact: J5 evidence checking is slow on mobile especially.
- Fix: collapse evidence by default, or show it in a side panel or popover from the chip. Add a "back to claim" link and a highlighted target.

**F5 (P2)**
- Screen/state: validation page Steps, mobile.
- Problem: the grid `2rem 1fr auto` keeps the pill + ms column wide, so step text and the JSON detail flow in a ~150 px column. The raw JSON detail is shown as body text.
- Principles: U5, U2, N2, N8.
- Evidence: S/c_j6_res390a.png, S/c_val1440.png.
- Impact: J6 result is hard to read and the "Snapshot" step spans about 1,000 px.
- Fix: under 600 px put the status under the title. Render the details as key-value pairs or a collapsible `<pre>`.

**F6 (P2)**
- Screen/state: narrow widths (320/390) and 768.
- Problem: `overflow-wrap:anywhere` on td, `.mono` and kv breaks ordinary tokens mid-word ("rpt_daily_collect|ions", "cost_usd_tot|al", "2026-|10-09").
- Principles: U2.
- Evidence: S/c_m_light_320_incidents.png, S/c_m_light_320_evals.png, S/combo_tablet.png.
- Impact: lower legibility and scanability.
- Fix: use `overflow-wrap:break-word` (break only when a token cannot fit), insert `<wbr>` after underscores, and set `white-space:nowrap` on dates.

**F7 (P2)**
- Screen/state: theme select.
- Problem: the theme choice is not persisted. A role switch (forced reload) or any reload resets it to System.
- Principles: S7, N3.
- Evidence: `states.mjs` log "theme after role switch reload: undefined system".
- Impact: users in dark mode get flashed back to light, which also breaks J8's role switch mid-task.
- Fix: store the theme in localStorage and apply it before the first paint. Avoid the full reload on role change.

**F8 (P3)**
- Screen/state: queued/running/cancelled investigation; Cancel button; proposal row; health note.
- Problem: missing vertical gaps:
  - the Cancel button flush with the state box;
  - the citation caveat flush with the empty box;
  - the proposal row against the next card;
  - the health note against the dl.
- Principles: U3.
- Evidence: S/combo_j4.png, S/combo_j8.png, S/s_viewer_rec_light_1440.png, S/m_light_1440_health.png.
- Fix: add `margin-top: var(--sp-3)` to `.action` after `.state`, a top margin to the muted caveat, and a bottom margin to `.proposal-row`.

**F9 (P3)**
- Screen/state: queued/cancelled investigation.
- Problem: three to four stacked empty dashed boxes (Citation check, Evidence (0), Tool calls) plus the Usage card are shown when there is nothing yet.
- Principles: N8, U1.
- Evidence: S/combo_j4.png, S/f_j8_after_cancel_390.png.
- Fix: hide these sections until there is data, or collapse them into one line.

**F10 (P3)**
- Screen/state: cross-screen consistency.
- Problems:
  - gallery pills are lowercase while the app uses Title Case;
  - the not-found page is left-aligned vs the centred state component;
  - the breadcrumb leaf shows the raw id while loading or on error vs "Incident";
  - invariant Before is text vs After as a pill;
  - key-value values mix mono 14 px and sans 16 px;
  - the gallery h1 is not focusable.
- Principles: N4, S1, U4.
- Evidence: S/c_gal1.png, S/combo_states390.png, S/c_val1440.png, S/c_inv_mid.png, `kbd2.mjs` log.
- Fix: use one label source (status.ts) in the gallery and one state component for the route 404, and normalise the dd font sizes.

**F11 (P3)**
- Screen/state: copy.
- Problems: "Proposal proposed", "Cost unknown (unknown)", "fake/—", "limit max_cost_usd", "Cancelled by dev:investigator". The empty-filter text blames "deterministic checks" even though manual reports exist.
- Principles: N2, U8.
- Evidence: S/s_viewer_rec_light_1440.png, S/c_inv_bot.png, S/combo_states390.png.
- Fix: write human labels ("Recovery proposed", "Model: fake (test double)", "Max cost: $1"), and use neutral empty copy ("No incidents with status Investigating").

**F12 (P3)**
- Screen/state: validation isolation checks.
- Problem: the detail text is truncated at the start ("ection failed:") or has a leading space.
- Principles: N9, U2.
- Evidence: S/c_val1440.png, S/c_j6_res390b.png.
- Fix: truncate at the end with an ellipsis, or show the full message in a disclosure.

**F13 (P3)**
- Screen/state: J6, 75 s run.
- Problem: all stages show "pending" until the run completes; there is no stage progress or elapsed time.
- Principles: N1, U10, S3.
- Evidence: S/c_j6_running.png, `flows.mjs` log (17:28:08→17:29:23).

**F14 (P3)**
- Screen/state: mobile nav and tabs.
- Problem: the active nav item keeps the desktop inset left bar on a horizontal chip. The nav plus top bar take about 175 px before content. The diagnosis tabs wrap onto two rows, and the selected underline sits on row 1.
- Principles: U5, U4.
- Evidence: S/c_inv390_top_dark.png, S/combo_states390.png.

**F15 (P3)**

**F16 (P3)**

- Fix: use `role="note"` or plain text for static permission notes, and give the gallery sections unique labels.

**F17 (P3)**

- Problem: the gallery does not cover button variants (primary, danger, disabled-with-reason), the inline confirm, the invalid textarea, the decision panel, the stale-refresh banner or the queued/running/cancelled cards. The token label wraps out of alignment.
- Principles: U4, N10.

- Evidence: S/combo_j4.png.
- Fix: show violations as the observed value, or label the columns per check type.

## 5. Limitations
- Single environment: Chromium only, at 100% zoom, with no real assistive technology. Focus and ARIA were inspected programmatically.
- Data shapes are limited: one pipeline, no restricted evidence, no live failed validation. Some states were reproduced by mocking with `page.route`.
- The 1024 and 768 widths were covered for screens in both themes but not for every error or loading state. The states were shot at 1440 and 390 in both themes.
- Mutations performed on my fixtures:
  - J4: started 2 investigations on inc_01a121ac5a79c8ebe81a (inv_01a121b5072b3522bd42, now awaiting review; inv_01a121b52e2cf35e2324, from the J8 "start another"). Then started inv_01a121b6c63e62e7e709 and cancelled it.
  - J6: validation run val_01a121b53d8e600c4322 passed.
  - J7: approved inv_01a121ac55227040c311 with a note, which resolved the incident inc_01a121ac550411a9d4bd.
- One subjective dimension: severity and score calibration reflect a visual and responsive specialist's weighting.
