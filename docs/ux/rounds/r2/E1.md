## What I covered

There are no colour-contrast violations in either theme. A 320 px reflow check on 7 routes found no horizontal scroll (`scrollWidth` = 320).
- **States.** Loading (4 s delay), HTTP 500, network abort, Retry recovery, 403, empty filter, and not-found (incident, investigation, validation, unknown route, malformed id) on 6 pages, in light/desktop and dark/mobile.
- **Reduced motion and keyboard.** Spinner animation becomes `none` under reduced motion. I also recorded keyboard-only tab order.
- **J4.** Run as investigator on my incident `inc_01a121ab50a7d1a2fd13`. It created `inv_01a121b567371210670a`, which stayed Queued for ~30 s and then reached Awaiting review / Abstained.
- **J8.** I started a second investigation (`inv_01a121b5ed1736997fc7`) through the "Start another" confirmation, then cancelled it while queued. I also tested back/forward and switching role mid-task.
- **J6.** Validated `prop_01a121ab4c553f1acfb4` in the sandbox. Run `val_01a121b6e9af60812336` went Queued → Running → Passed in ~53 s.
- **J7.** As approver on `inv_01a121ab4c553f1acfb4` I tried, in order:
  1. Reject with an empty note.
  2. Reject with a whitespace-only note.
  3. A valid note, then "Go back" from the reject confirmation.
  4. Approve with the note and with "also resolve" unchecked, so the shared incident was left alone.

  I also checked what viewer and investigator see.

## Limitations

- Other evaluators were mutating the shared environment during my run (for example, the open count went from 26 to 23), and worker load may have stretched queue times.
- The "running" state and restricted evidence were seen only through mocks.
- No real screen reader or device was used. Mobile is Chromium emulation only.
- I did not approve an unvalidated proposal or resolve the shared incident, to respect the fixture boundaries. F5 is based on the rendered approver card plus the code.
- My test harness injects the role into localStorage on load. The role-switch test was redone without that injection (misc3) to avoid that artefact.
- Scores are my own calibrated judgement under the brief's scale; the brief notes that this rubric is not an official Nielsen or Shneiderman scale.
